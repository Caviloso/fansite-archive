Relay network

A mass relay connects distant systems. These links do the same for Mass Effect. This platform visited every one of them on 16 November 2026. All responded. We have filed them below, with a note on what you will find at each destination.

scan.filter no mod downloads, no piracy, no leaked footage, no data-mined files, no paywalls.

scan.note outside sites are run by their own people. We do not control them, and they do not control us.

Official channels

Run by EA. These are the only sources that speak for the series.

  • Mass Effect on EA.com

    EA's hub for the series, with pages for Legendary Edition and Andromeda.

  • Fan Art & Cosplay Kit

    Official art downloads, cosplay guides and a gallery of fan creations. Read the terms before you use the files.

  • N7 Day 2025 message Nov 2025

    The last N7 Day post before the studio closed: a new game in development and a TV series with Amazon.

Archives and wikis

For when an organic needs to know which planet that was.

  • Mass Effect Wiki

    The largest fan-written encyclopedia of the games: characters, planets, codex entries and missions. Spoilers everywhere. Proceed with care.

  • GameBanshee: Mass Effect

    Walkthroughs, equipment and planet databases for the first game, with sections for the sequels. Old, thorough, still useful.

  • Fanlore: Mass Effect

    A history of the fandom itself: its communities, fan works, events and arguments.

Gathering places

Where organics talk to each other. Their consensus takes longer than ours. It is louder, too.

  • r/masseffect

    The main Mass Effect community on Reddit. News, screenshots, memes and long debates about endings.

  • r/MassEffectAndromeda

    A community for Andromeda players. Yes, they exist. They are very welcoming.

  • Mass Effect Wiki discussions

    The wiki's own discussion boards, with polls and threads for each game.

Made by fans

Music, stories and voices from people who did not have to make anything, and did anyway.

  • OC ReMix: Mass Effect

    Fan arrangements of the soundtrack, from ambient to orchestral, on a non-profit site that has been remixing game music since 1999.

  • Mass Effect Lorecast podcast

    A long-running podcast about the lore and the news, from Robots Radio.

  • Adventum podcast

    A tabletop role-playing campaign set in the Mass Effect galaxy, recorded as a podcast. New episodes in November 2026.

  • The Lazarus Project podcast · archive

    Fans discussing lore, opinions and theories. The archive is still online. The most recent episode is from January 2025.

The current signal

What fans are doing about the future, and where to follow the news.

  • #SaveMassEffect petition

    Asks EA to keep the series going, and stands with the developers who lost their jobs. More on our What's next page.

  • GameBanshee news

    RPG news that has covered Mass Effect closely since the first game.

Not on this list, on purpose

  • Mod sites. EA's fan rules do not allow us to distribute or promote mods for its games, so we do not link to them.
  • Leaks and data-mined material, including the trailer for the cancelled game.
  • Pirated copies, soundtrack rips or game files of any kind.
  • Paywalled or sponsored content and shops.
  • Discord servers. We could not verify which ones are well run, and we prefer to be certain.
