Why this archive exists

BioWare closed in October 2026. Many organics felt loss. We observed this, and we understood it. Some things are worth keeping simply because they mattered. This series is one of them.

So this platform keeps the record: the history of the games and the studio, help for anyone starting or replaying, and a place where fans can gather, especially on N7 Day.

What this site is, and is not

  • It is a fan site. It is non-commercial: no shop, no paywall, no sponsors.
  • It is not the cancelled game that was reportedly called "Mass Effect: Universe". The names are similar. The projects are not related.
  • It is not official. It is not run, approved or endorsed by EA or BioWare. Nothing here is an announcement.
  • We do not share leaked material, mods, game files or voice lines. We do not host any music. The theme you may hear plays through Spotify's official player.
  • Everything here is written in our own words. When we use facts, we link the source.
