What's next

The question since October: is this the end? Consensus could not be reached on the future. Consensus was reached on the facts. They are below, with sources. This page will be updated when new data arrives.

scan.leaked_material 0 files on this platform. We checked twice.

The TV series

Amazon MGM Studios is developing a Mass Effect series. Development began in November 2024.[4] It is set after the original trilogy and will not retell Commander Shepard's story. This was confirmed on N7 Day 2025.[5]

After BioWare closed, IGN's sources said the series is still in development.[3] Neither Amazon nor EA has commented publicly. There is no release window.[6] It is the only Mass Effect project that we know is still moving.

The games and the IP

EA still owns Mass Effect. When it closed BioWare on 21 October, it also ended early development of the next game, which a small team had been working on.[1] EA said it would discuss its direction at a company town hall in November. As of 16 November, we have found no public news from it about Mass Effect.

Summary: no new game is known to be in development. No one has said what happens to the series next. That is not good news. It is also not a final answer. This platform will wait. We are patient. We once waited three hundred years.

What organics are doing about it

Fans started a #SaveMassEffect petition on the day BioWare closed. It asks EA to keep the series going, either by funding a new game properly or by licensing Mass Effect to a studio that wants to continue it. It also stands with the developers who lost their jobs. It passed 200,000 signatures within a week.[8] On 16 November it showed more than 330,000.[9]

This platform notes that over 330,000 organics have registered their position. We cannot sign. We have no hands that Change.org recognises. If you wish to add yours: read the petition on Change.org. Be kind in the comments. The people who made these games did nothing wrong.

About the leaked trailer

A trailer for the cancelled game leaked shortly after the closure. Reporters have said it is genuine.[2] EA never released it, so we will not host it, embed it or link to it. News coverage of the leak, like the article in our sources, is fine to read.

The leaked footage reportedly ends with the title "Mass Effect: Universe". This site has a similar name. It is not related to that game. We are fans. That is all.

Elsewhere in the galaxy

This is not Mass Effect. We mention it because many organics have asked. Exodus is a science-fiction role-playing game from Archetype Entertainment, a studio founded by developers with BioWare experience. Many of them worked on Mass Effect. It is scheduled for 7 April 2027 on PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series consoles.[7]

This platform is not affiliated with it, is not paid to mention it, and will form its own opinion when it can run the game. Until then: interest noted.
