Gather · Tool 02

Which squadmate are you?

Nine queries. Answer as yourself, not as Shepard-Commander. This platform will compare your answers with everything it has observed about the crew, and reach consensus.

There are no right answers and no score. This is a personality quiz, not a game. Results are spoiler-light: we describe who they are, never what happens to them.
