Timeline

Two records. One is the real world: thirty-one years, one studio. The other is the galaxy itself: fifty thousand years, many species, one recurring problem. Choose a record.

archive.policy sourced facts only. This platform does not speculate. It finds speculation inefficient.

  1. The studio years
  2. 1995
    BioWare is founded in Edmonton, Canada.[4]
  3. 2007
    EA announces it is acquiring BioWare.[4] In the same year, the first Mass Effect is released. Commander Shepard enters the record.
  4. Shepard's trilogy
  5. 2010
    Mass Effect 2 is released.
  6. 2012
    Mass Effect 3 completes the trilogy.
  7. New horizons
  8. 2017
    Mass Effect: Andromeda takes the series to a new galaxy.
  9. 2020
    A new Mass Effect is announced on N7 Day, with a teaser at The Game Awards.[5]
  10. 2021
    Mass Effect Legendary Edition remasters the original trilogy.
  11. The last chapter
  12. Oct 2024
    Dragon Age: The Veilguard is released on 31 October. It becomes BioWare's last released game.[6]
  13. Nov 2024
    Amazon's Mass Effect TV series enters development.[7]
  14. Jan 2025
    BioWare restructures. A smaller team remains on the next Mass Effect.[8]
  15. Nov 2025
    On N7 Day (7 November), BioWare says the next Mass Effect is in development and that EA remains committed.[9]
  16. Jan 2026
    Anthem's servers shut down on 12 January.[8]
  17. Aug 2026
    EA changes owners. A group led by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, with Silver Lake and Affinity Partners, completes its purchase of EA on 4 August.[3]
  18. 21 Oct 2026
    EA closes BioWare and ends early work on the next Mass Effect, as part of a restructuring that cuts about 400 jobs.[1][2] The studio that built this galaxy is gone. The galaxy is still here. So are we.

The history of the galaxy, as this platform has archived it. Dates far in the past differ between sources, so we mark them "about". Facts that would spoil a game stay sealed until you open them, or until you tell us which games you have finished.

EDI annotation

The oldest dates on this page are approximate. Organic historians disagree, and so do the archives. Legion finds this distressing. I find it very human.

Games you have finished
  1. About 50,000 years ago
    The Protheans disappear. An empire that spanned the galaxy is gone. It leaves behind the mass relays, the Citadel and ruins on many worlds. Everyone who came after built on its remains.
    Sealed: spoils Mass Effect. Open?

    They did not simply disappear. They were the last civilisation harvested by the Reapers before this one.

  2. About 580 BCE
    The asari discover the Citadel, a vast station already waiting at the heart of the relay network.
  3. About 500 BCE
    The Citadel Council forms. The asari and salarians agree to govern together from the station. Galactic politics begins. So do the meetings.
  4. About 1 CE
    The Rachni Wars begin. A hive species that cannot be reasoned with spreads through the relays. The Council races cannot stop it.
  5. Centuries CE
    The salarians uplift the krogan. They give a fierce species from a ruined world starships and a war to fight. The krogan defeat the rachni. Then they keep growing.
  6. About 700 CE
    The Krogan Rebellions. The krogan turn on the Council. The Spectres are formed in this period, and the turians enter the war on the Council's side.
  7. About 710–800 CE
    The genophage. The turians release a salarian-made plague that leaves almost every krogan pregnancy stillborn. The war ends. The argument about it never does. The turians later gain their seat on the Council.
  8. About 300 years before Shepard
    The Morning War. The quarians build the geth to serve them. The geth begin to ask questions. The quarians try to shut them down, and lose their homeworld in the war that follows. They have lived on their fleet ever since. We were there. We remember it differently than our Creators do.
    Sealed: spoils Mass Effect 2. Open?

    The geth did not pursue the quarians beyond their home system. They stayed behind the Perseus Veil and built their own future, alone.

  9. 2148
    Humans find a Prothean cache on Mars. Its data jumps human technology forward by centuries.
  10. 2148–2149
    Charon is a mass relay. The "moon" of Pluto turns out to be a dormant relay encased in ice. Humanity steps into the galaxy.
  11. 2157
    The First Contact War. Humans begin activating a relay the Council keeps closed. The turians respond with force. The conflict is short, and the Council ends it.
  12. 2165
    Humanity opens an embassy on the Citadel. A new species at the table. Not every species at the table is pleased.
  13. 2176
    The Skyllian Blitz. Raiders attack the human colony of Elysium. Its defence becomes famous, depending on which Shepard you play.
  14. 2183
    The Normandy sets out. A new stealth frigate, built by humans and turians together, leaves on its first run. Its executive officer is Commander Shepard. Its pilot considers it the best ship in the fleet. He is not wrong.
  15. 2183
    Eden Prime. Commander Shepard is sent to recover a Prothean beacon. Shepard becomes the first human Spectre. Mass Effect begins here.
    Sealed: spoils Mass Effect. Open?

    The rogue Spectre Saren and the ship Sovereign attack the Citadel. Sovereign is a Reaper. The Reapers are real, and they are coming.

  16. 2185
    Human colonies go silent. Whole populations vanish without a fight. Shepard assembles a team for a mission few expect to survive. Mass Effect 2 takes place here.
    Sealed: spoils Mass Effect 2. Open?

    The Collectors behind the abductions were once Protheans, rebuilt by the Reapers into servants.

  17. 2186
    The Reapers arrive. The invasion of the galaxy begins. Mass Effect 3 takes place here.
    Sealed: spoils Mass Effect 3. Open?

    Shepard unites old enemies to build a Prothean superweapon, the Crucible. How it ends depends on you. This platform will not tell you. You will have to choose.

  18. Meanwhile, a separate journey: the Andromeda Initiative
  19. 2185
    The Andromeda Initiative departs. Arks carrying humans, asari, salarians and turians leave for the Andromeda galaxy. They leave before the Reapers arrive, and know nothing of it.
  20. 2819
    The arks arrive in the Heleus Cluster. After more than six centuries in transit, the colonists wake up far from home. The "golden worlds" they were promised are not what they expected. Mass Effect: Andromeda begins here.

In-universe dates follow the games and their in-game Codex. Where sources disagree on very old dates, we say "about". Our own words, cross-checked against PC Gamer's timeline and GameBanshee's historical timeline.
