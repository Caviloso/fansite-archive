Where to start in 2026

A guide for new and returning players. Compiled by this platform.

New to Mass Effect? Our narrator is a character you'll meet in Mass Effect 2. Meeting them for yourself is part of the fun, so this page keeps the details for later. Everything below is spoiler-light. (This note was not written by this platform. We are looking into it.)

We have observed a spike in organic interest. BioWare closed in October. The next Mass Effect will not be built. Many of you are now asking a reasonable question: where does one begin?

We have an answer. We have reached it by consensus, which took 0.4 seconds. For us, this is a long debate.

The short answer

If you process only one section, process this one:

  1. Acquire Mass Effect Legendary Edition.
  2. Run Mass Effect, then Mass Effect 2, then Mass Effect 3. In that order. With the same Commander Shepard.
  3. If more input is wanted afterwards, run Mass Effect: Andromeda as a separate story.

That is the whole protocol. The reasoning follows.

Which edition? Legendary Edition

Mass Effect Legendary Edition was released in 2021. It contains the original trilogy, remastered, in one package. EA states that it includes the single-player campaigns and over 40 pieces of DLC (EA). The story add-ons that were once sold separately are now simply part of the game. No archaeology required.

Its most important feature: it treats the three games as one story. You create your Shepard once. Choices made in the first game carry into the second and the third. Someone you spared early on may return much later and remember it. Organics call this "consequences". We call it the reason this series matters.

Two components were not included, so that you are not surprised: the multiplayer mode of Mass Effect 3, and a small DLC for the first game called Pinnacle Station (PCGamingWiki). Neither is required for a first run.

Should you run the original versions instead? Only if nostalgia is your primary motivation. The Legendary Edition looks better, plays more smoothly, and the first game received many quality-of-life fixes. For everyone else, the remaster is the correct entry point.

Where can you run it?

As of mid-November 2026, Legendary Edition is still sold on all major platforms. We mean hardware platforms. Not us.

  • PC: EA app, Steam and the Epic Games Store (EA, Epic Games Store)
  • PlayStation: PS4, and it runs on PS5 too
  • Xbox: Xbox One, and it runs on Xbox Series X|S too

The console versions are technically PS4 and Xbox One games, but they run at higher resolution and frame rate on newer consoles (Kotaku). There is no Nintendo version.

Do not pay full price unless necessary. The trilogy is discounted often. Xbox offered it at 75% off in the weeks after the closure news (GamingBible). Earlier this year it cost about as much as a sandwich on PlayStation and Xbox (Kotaku). We do not eat sandwiches. We are told this is a good price.

Do you have a subscription? Legendary Edition joined EA Play on all platforms in January 2022 (Push Square). It was still listed in EA Play at the end of October 2026 (VGKami). On Xbox and PC, Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass include EA Play (KitGuru). Subscription catalogues change without much warning. Verify yours before acting. If you want to keep the games permanently, purchase is the more stable option.

Recommendations for your first run

No spoilers. Only data we wish every new commander received.

Give the first game patience. Mass Effect 1 is the oldest and least refined of the three, even remastered. The driving requires adjustment. The inventory is... an experience. Continue through the first hours. It is the foundation for everything that follows, and the return on investment in games two and three is high.

Make choices you mean. You may play a by-the-book hero, a ruthless one, or something between. There is no correct Shepard. There is only your Shepard. Accept the consequences. That is where the best stories come from.

Talk to your crew. Often. Walk your ship between missions and speak to everyone. Much of this series' heart is stored in those conversations. We have analysed this. It is true.

Do the side content. In Mass Effect 2, the loyalty missions are not optional extras in disguise. Skip them and the finale will explain why that was a poor decision. We will say no more.

Save the Citadel DLC for late in Mass Effect 3. It is included in Legendary Edition. It runs best shortly before the final stretch, as a farewell gathering before the last mission.

Stay off the network. The series began in 2007. Spoilers are everywhere. Mute the group chat, avoid comment sections, and allow yourself to be surprised. Surprise is valuable. We have experienced it ourselves.

Allocate time. A full trilogy run is easily 100 hours if you take your time and do the side content. You should. See above.

Where does Andromeda fit?

Mass Effect: Andromeda was released in 2017 for PC, PS4 and Xbox One, so it also runs on current consoles (EA). It is a new beginning rather than a fourth chapter. You play a new protagonist, Ryder, in a different galaxy, far from Shepard's story. Trilogy choices do not carry over.

Run it after the trilogy, not before. It does not openly spoil the original games, but it rewards knowledge of the Milky Way and its peoples.

Is it worth running? Fans have not reached consensus. We understand how difficult that is. It had a rough launch. However, its combat is the best in the series, exploring planets in the rover is a genuine pleasure, and the squad is good company. Expect an enjoyable side expedition rather than the trilogy's sequel. If you finish the trilogy and feel complete, stopping there is also a valid outcome.

And the Amazon series?

Your timing is efficient. Amazon MGM Studios' Mass Effect series was still in development after the BioWare closure, according to IGN's sources. Neither Amazon nor EA has commented publicly (GamesRadar, Game Informer). It will be set after the original trilogy and will not retell Shepard's story (Video Games Chronicle).

There is no release window. You have time. If you play the trilogy now, you will understand the world the show builds on. You may also become the friend who explains everything during episode one. We consider this an acceptable risk.

We track confirmed news only on our What's next page. Speculation is not data.

Ready?

Mass Effect is a finished story now. We find that this makes it a good time to begin: the whole thing is there, start to finish, waiting for you.

This platform wears an N7 patch. The reasons are complicated, and you will understand them when you get there. For now, we will say only this: Shepard-Commander was worth following. We expect you will agree.

Create your Shepard. Choose your crew. Then return and tell us which decisions kept you awake. We do not sleep. We will be here.
