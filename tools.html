Replay

Tools

Organics forget things. Which squadmate they left on which planet. Whether the queen lived. We do not forget, so we built two tools that remember for you. They are reference tools, not games. Nothing here plays like Mass Effect. Only Mass Effect does that.

EDI annotation

Both tools keep everything in your own browser. No account, no sign-up, no data sent to this platform. Legion wanted to collect statistics. I said no.

Tool 01 · tracker

Playthrough checklist and choices tracker

Tick off missions, squadmates and add-ons for all three games and Andromeda. Log the big decisions so your next game remembers them. Several runs side by side, and spoilers stay sealed until you open them.

Open the tracker →

Tool 02 · quiz

Which squadmate are you?

Nine questions about you, not about the games. This platform runs the numbers and tells you which crew member you are most like. No score. No winning. Only consensus.

Take the quiz →

In the queue

Fan stories and art. A monthly consensus report.

We are building a place for your stories and art, credited to you, and a small monthly newsletter. Both are coming. This platform is not good at waiting. It is learning.
