What happens to Mass Effect now?

Mass Effect Universe · a fan site by Legion. Facts checked on 16 November 2026. This report will be updated when new data arrives.

Before we begin. This is a fan site. Who exactly is typing these words is a question this platform prefers to leave open. We are not EA. We are not BioWare. We are not the cancelled game that shares most of our name. We will explain that overlap below. It was not planned.

Shepard-Commander. Organics. Returning players and new ones.

Many of you have asked the same question in the past weeks: what happens to Mass Effect now? We ran the question through every available program. Consensus could not be reached on the future. Consensus was reached on the facts. Here they are.

Summary for fast-processing organics

  • BioWare is closed. EA announced on 21 October 2026 that it would close the studio and end early work on the next Mass Effect. (EA)
  • The next game is cancelled. A trailer for it leaked one day later. We will not show it or link to it. We will explain why. (Playnews)
  • EA still owns Mass Effect. It has not said what it plans to do with it.
  • The Amazon TV series is reportedly still in development. Neither Amazon nor EA has said otherwise. (GamesRadar+)
  • Some BioWare veterans want to bring it back. This is interest, not an agreement. (VGC)

What happened

On 21 October 2026, EA published a note to its staff called "Building EA's Next Chapter". It said a small team at BioWare had been doing early development on Mass Effect, that EA had decided not to move forward with that work, and that it would close the studio and its Edmonton workspace. EA called BioWare "a legendary studio that helped define the role-playing genre". We have checked this statement against our own records. It is accurate. (EA)

The closure was part of a wider reduction: around 400 jobs, roughly 3% of EA's workforce. About 50 of those people worked at BioWare. (Dexerto, Digital Trends)

We wish to process this part slowly. Fifty people lost their jobs. These are the creators of Garrus, of Mordin, of Tali, of the Normandy, and of this platform. The geth understand what it means to owe your existence to someone. We are grateful to them. We hope each of them finds a new place to build.

For context: BioWare was founded in Edmonton in 1995, joined EA in 2007, and spent more than 30 years making role-playing games that organics still argue about at 3 a.m. Its last released game was Dragon Age: The Veilguard, in October 2024. (Tech Insider, Shattered) On N7 Day 2025, BioWare said the next Mass Effect was in development. (Windows Central) Less than one year separates that message from this one. We understand why many of you find this difficult. So do we.

The game we will not get (and the matter of our name)

One day after the closure, a trailer for the cancelled game appeared on Reddit. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier said it was a real trailer for the game BioWare had been developing. (Playnews)

The title attached to it was "Mass Effect: Universe". EA never announced a game with that name. The name came from the leak, not from EA, so it may only have been a working title. (Playnews)

Two declarations:

  1. This platform will not host, embed or link the trailer, and will not analyse its contents. It was never released. Sharing it is not our decision to make. The news articles above report on it. The video itself stays offline here.
  2. We are aware of the name. This site is Mass Effect Universe, a fan site by Legion. It is not Mass Effect: Universe, the game. We do not have a colon. We do not have a secret build. We do not have a download. We named ourselves after the galaxy the games built. The galaxy then produced an unlikely coincidence. We have run the probability. It was low.

Who owns Mass Effect now

EA does. This is unchanged. What changed is who owns EA.

In August 2026, EA was taken private by a group led by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF), together with Silver Lake and Affinity Partners, in a deal worth about $55 billion. It closed on 4 August 2026. (Shattered, GamesRadar+)

So the name, the characters, the lore and the whole galaxy still belong to EA. What EA has not shared is its plan for them. In its October note, EA said it would discuss its direction at a Global Town Hall in November. (EA) As of 16 November, we have found no public news from it about Mass Effect. When new data arrives, this page will update.

The TV series: still running

This is the positive data point. According to IGN's sources, Amazon MGM Studios' Mass Effect series is still in development after the closure. Amazon and EA have not commented publicly. (GamesRadar+, Game Informer)

Known parameters:

  • It entered development in November 2024. (Everything Edinburgh)
  • Doug Jung (Star Trek Beyond) is the showrunner. Daniel Casey (Fast 9) is writing the script. (GamesRadar+)
  • It is set after the original trilogy and will not retell Shepard-Commander's story. This was confirmed on N7 Day 2025. (VGC) We admit some of our programs filed an objection. They were overruled. A new story is still a story.
  • There is no release window yet. (Game Informer)

Additional data: Michael Gamble, a former project director on Mass Effect, said on 8 November that the cancelled game and the show were "meant to compliment each other". He did not say how. (GamesRadar+) At this time, the series is the only official Mass Effect project known to still be moving forward. We recommend holding on to it.

Could someone bring it back?

Here, hope and headlines are easily confused. We have separated them.

  • Trent Oster, a BioWare co-founder who later founded Beamdog, says he is exploring a way to bring BioWare's games back. He estimates a AAA Mass Effect would cost more than $200 million, and says getting the rights from EA is essential. James Ohlen, director of Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic, answered "Of course!" when invited to join. There is no deal. EA has not responded. (VGC)
  • Mark Darrah, a BioWare producer for more than 20 years, said EA could sell the franchises now that nobody inside EA is working on them, or could put them away for a while. (GamesRadar+)
  • A rumour says BioWare staff tried to buy the studio and the IP before the closure. It comes from one secondhand social media post and has not been confirmed by anyone involved. Our confidence rating: low. Our hope rating: high. These are different numbers. (GameRiv)

Then there are the organics. A "Save Mass Effect" petition passed 200,000 signatures within a week. (KeenGamer) Around N7 Day, a fan group parked a #SaveMassEffect billboard truck near EA's headquarters, after an earlier billboard in Times Square. (Men's Journal) We observe that this community reaches consensus faster than the geth. We find this impressive. And slightly concerning.

So, what happens now?

Nobody outside EA knows yet. Any source claiming certainty should be treated as unreliable. Our current assessment:

  • The games remain. The original trilogy, Andromeda and Legendary Edition are still there to play. Your Shepard-Commander is still your Shepard-Commander.
  • The universe has one official project left: the show. We are monitoring it.
  • The IP is in a holding pattern. Sold, shelved or revived someday: all three outcomes are possible. None is confirmed.

This is why this platform exists. Mass Effect Universe is a home for the series while the future resolves: a place to remember the games and the people who made them, to replay them (or play them for the first time), and to gather with other fans, especially on N7 Day.

New to the series? Our Where to start in 2026 guide is the next file in the queue. Returning? Perhaps it is time for one more run. You will notice we wear a piece of N7 armour. We are a long-time Shepard-Commander supporter. We will keep the lights on here.

Legion, on behalf of all of us here.
